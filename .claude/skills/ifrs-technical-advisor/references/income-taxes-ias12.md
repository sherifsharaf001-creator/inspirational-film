# IAS 12 — Income Taxes

## Two pieces
- **Current tax** — tax payable/recoverable on taxable profit for the period at
  enacted or **substantively enacted** rates. Recognize unpaid current tax as a
  liability, overpaid as an asset.
- **Deferred tax** — the future tax effect of **temporary differences** using the
  balance-sheet liability method.

## Temporary differences
Difference between the **carrying amount** of an asset/liability and its **tax
base** (the amount attributed for tax purposes).
- **Taxable temporary differences** → **deferred tax liabilities** (future taxable
  amounts): e.g., accelerated tax depreciation, an asset revalued up with no tax-
  base change, capitalized development costs deducted for tax when incurred.
- **Deductible temporary differences** → **deferred tax assets** (future
  deductible amounts): e.g., provisions/accruals not yet tax-deductible, unused
  tax losses and credits carried forward, ECL allowances.

Tax base examples: a receivable of 100 already taxed has a tax base of 100 (no
temp diff); interest received taxed on a cash basis has a tax base of 0 on the
accrued receivable → taxable temporary difference.

## Recognition rules and exceptions
- Recognize **all** taxable temporary differences as DTLs, and deductible ones and
  loss carryforwards as **DTAs only to the extent future taxable profit is
  probable** to use them. Reassess unrecognized DTAs each period.
- **Initial recognition exception:** do **not** recognize deferred tax on the
  initial recognition of an asset/liability in a transaction that is not a
  business combination and affects neither accounting nor taxable profit — but
  note the amendment removing this exemption where the transaction gives rise to
  **equal and offsetting** taxable and deductible temporary differences (e.g.,
  leases and decommissioning provisions): recognize both the DTA and DTL.
- **Goodwill:** no deferred tax on the initial recognition of goodwill.
- **Investments in subsidiaries/associates/branches/JVs:** don't recognize a DTL
  on undistributed profits where the parent controls the timing of reversal and
  reversal is not probable in the foreseeable future.

## Measurement
Use the tax rate **expected to apply when the asset is realized/liability is
settled**, based on enacted or substantively enacted rates — reflecting the
**expected manner of recovery** (use vs. sale can carry different rates, e.g.,
investment property, or capital vs. income rates). Deferred tax is **not
discounted**. DTAs/DTLs are always **non-current** on the balance sheet.

## Where the tax goes (backwards tracing)
Recognize tax consistently with the item it relates to:
- item in **P&L** → tax in P&L;
- item in **OCI** (e.g., revaluation surplus, FVOCI, cash flow hedge) → tax in
  OCI;
- item in **equity** (e.g., some share-based payment, compound instrument split)
  → tax in equity;
- arising in a **business combination** → affects goodwill/bargain-purchase gain.

## Offsetting and Pillar Two
Offset current tax assets/liabilities only with a legally enforceable right and
intent to settle net; DTAs/DTLs offset only if they relate to the same taxable
entity/authority. For **OECD Pillar Two** global minimum tax, IAS 12 provides a
mandatory temporary **exception** — do not recognize or disclose deferred taxes
related to Pillar Two top-up taxes; disclose current Pillar Two tax expense
separately.

## Illustrative calculation & entry
Equipment cost 100; accounting depreciation to date 20 (carrying 80); tax
depreciation to date 40 (tax base 60). Taxable temporary difference = 80 − 60 =
20. Tax rate 25% → DTL 5.
```
Income tax expense (deferred)      5
  Deferred tax liability                5
```
Effective-tax-rate reconciliation between the expected (statutory × pre-tax
profit) and actual tax charge is a required disclosure — build it to explain
permanent differences, rate changes, and unrecognized DTAs.
